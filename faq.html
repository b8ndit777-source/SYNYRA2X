About IEMs

What is an IEM?

IEM stands for In-Ear Monitor. IEMs are earphones designed to sit inside the ear canal and deliver detailed sound directly to the listener.

Are IEMs good for gaming?

They can be. Many IEMs provide detailed sound and can make it easier to hear positional cues such as footsteps, reloads and other in-game sounds.

Are IEMs better than gaming headsets?

It depends on what you prefer. IEMs can offer excellent detail and a lightweight design, while traditional gaming headsets may offer features such as built-in microphones and larger ear cups.

Do I need an amplifier or DAC?

Most entry-level IEMs can be driven directly from common phones, computers and gaming devices. Some higher-end setups may benefit from additional audio equipment.

There isn't one answer for everyone. Different IEMs have different sound signatures. Once our product collection is finalized, we'll explain what each recommendation is best suited for.

What does "sound signature" mean?

Sound signature describes the overall character of an IEM's sound. For example, an IEM may emphasize bass, vocals, treble or provide a more balanced presentation.
